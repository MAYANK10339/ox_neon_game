OX NEON v2.0 - ANDROID APK INSTALL GUIDE
========================================
1. Choose your APK:
   - OX_Neon_Game_v2.apk (Recommended: Voice Chat, Text Chat, 3x3, 5x5, 10x10 grids, ~231 KB)
   - OX_Neon_Game_v1.apk (Classic 3x3 edition preserved, ~223 KB)
2. Transfer to your Android phone via WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive, or USB cable.
3. Tap the APK file to install:
   - If updating from v1, tap "Update". Your settings and scores are preserved.
   - If installing for the first time, allow "Install unknown apps" when prompted.
4. Voice Chat: When playing Multiplayer online, hold the Mic button at the bottom to send voice notes!
